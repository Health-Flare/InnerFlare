# Inner Flare 1.2: short-form video scripts

Five vertical (9:16) videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
Plan, recording setup, and claims guardrails are in [`README.md`](README.md);
read the guardrails before changing a line of voiceover.

**Script format:** each beat has a timecode, what's on screen (**SCREEN**),
the burned-in caption or title (**TEXT**), and the spoken line (**VO**).
Voiceover is optional. Every video is written to work muted, with the
captions carrying the whole message. If you record VO, use one calm, plain
voice with no hype read and no music drop on the hook.

**Music:** low, unobtrusive, royalty-free. Nothing trending with lyrics; it
dates fast and fights the captions.

Timecodes are targets. If a screen action takes longer on a real device,
let it breathe and trim the talking, not the demo.

## 2. It says "I don't know" (30s)

**Pillar:** Honest by default
**For:** the sceptic with irregular cycles who has been burned by confident
predictions. This is the strongest differentiator in the release; make it
well.

| Time | SCREEN | TEXT | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Black, then a generic calendar graphic with one date circled confidently. (Not a real competitor's UI.) | Your period app is guessing. | Your period app is guessing. |
| 0:03–0:06 | Cut to Inner Flare. | Mine admits it. | Mine tells me when it is. |
| 0:06–0:13 | **Fresh install, one or two logged periods.** Gauge card set to "Estimated days until next period" showing its range state and the "A rough range: not enough cycle history yet…" line. Zoom on the text. | Not enough history? You get a labelled range. Not a fake date. | With only a cycle or two logged, it shows a rough range, and it says that's what it is. |
| 0:13–0:19 | Same install. Trend card showing "Not enough cycles logged yet…". | Fewer than two complete cycles? No trend. | And it won't draw a trend out of one data point. |
| 0:19–0:25 | Cut to the demo-data install. Gauge with a real value; trend line chart with the average reference line. | Once there's enough data, it shows its work: scaled to your average, not a textbook 28 days. | Once it has enough, it shows you the number, measured against your own average, not a textbook twenty-eight days. |
| 0:25–0:30 | End card. | Inner Flare 1.2. Estimates, clearly labelled. | Inner Flare. Honest estimates, on your phone only. |

**Caption:**
> A cycle tracker that says "not enough data yet" when that's the truth.
> Inner Flare's charts scale to your own cycles and never invent a trend.
> Estimates, not a diagnosis.

**Hashtags:** #irregularperiods #PCOS #cycletracking #periodtracker #privacy

**Notes:**
- You need two installs (or a clean wipe between shots): one with one or two
  logged periods for the honest-empty states, one with demo data.
- Irregular cycles also trigger the range state (last three cycles differing
  by more than 7 days). If you'd rather show that than a new user, log three
  periods with gaps like 24, 38, 29 days on the fresh install.
- Tagging #PCOS is fine; **do not** say or imply the app helps manage or
  detect PCOS. It shows logged data, nothing more.
- The opening "guessing" graphic must be generic. Don't show or name any
  real app.

## 4. Nothing to sign up for (20s)

**Pillar:** Private, still
**For:** the privacy-first audience, and anyone who's uninstalled an app at
the "create an account" screen.

| Time | SCREEN | TEXT | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Text only. | The first thing this app asks you for: | The first thing this app asks you for… |
| 0:03–0:05 | Text only, hold a beat. | Nothing. | …nothing. |
| 0:05–0:12 | Fresh install: unlock screen → Face ID/fingerprint → first-run privacy and disclaimer screen. Slow scroll through it. | No account. No email. No password. Your logs stay on your phone unless you export them. | No account, no email, no password. Your logs stay on your phone unless you choose to export them. |
| 0:12–0:16 | Hold on the "not a medical or diagnostic device" line. | And it tells you straight: it's not a medical device. Estimates are estimates. | And it tells you plainly what it isn't: a medical device. |
| 0:16–0:20 | End card. | Inner Flare. No internet permission. Free and open source. | Inner Flare. It doesn't even have internet permission. |

**Caption:**
> No account. No cloud. No internet permission. Inner Flare 1.2 tells you
> all of that on first launch, in plain words, and then gets out of the way.
> Free and open source.

**Hashtags:** #privacy #dataprivacy #periodtracker #opensource #cycletracking

**Notes:**
- Record on a device with biometrics enrolled so the unlock is one tap.
- Don't tap the privacy policy link on camera; it leaves the app for the
  browser, which muddies "no internet" for viewers even though it's the
  browser, not the app, making the request.
- "No internet permission" is literally true on Android (no `INTERNET`
  permission in the manifest). iOS has no equivalent permission, so the
  accurate iOS phrasing is "makes no network requests." If this video is
  cut for an iOS-only placement, use that line instead.

## Reusing footage

Record once, cut five times. A single session covers almost everything:

1. **Demo-data install:** grid, customize/resize, add gauge, add trend,
   bar/line toggle, cycle detail table, Auto-lock screen, log-a-day chart
   refresh.
2. **Fresh install (one or two logged periods):** first-run disclaimer,
   gauge range state, trend "not enough cycles" state.

Capture each action three times so the editor has a clean take.
