## Full release notes

### How estimates work, with sources

Every estimate Inner Flare shows is a plain-statistics calculation from
your own logged dates, with one published assumption behind it (average
cycle length, typical period length, the gap between ovulation and your
next period). Those assumptions now have a home:

- A new **"How estimates work"** screen lists each estimate (average cycle
  length and variability, predicted next period, predicted fertile window,
  irregular-cycle detection), how it's calculated, and the full citation
  and link to the original source for any number that isn't purely your
  own data.
- Reachable from an info button on the Insights screen, a "How is this
  calculated?" link under your predictions, the Calendar legend, and
  Settings > About.
- The fertile-window caveat on Insights now says plainly that ovulation is
  assumed to fall about 14 days before your next period, that this really
  ranges from about 7 to 19 days, and that it's a rough guide, not a
  method of contraception, instead of a single generic disclaimer.
- Sources link out to the original publication (not a copy on our own
  site); opening one is an explicit tap that hands the address to your
  system browser. The app itself still makes no network calls.

### App version shown in Settings > About

Settings > About now shows the installed app version, useful when
reporting an issue or confirming you're on the latest build.

### Under the hood

Android toolchain updates (Gradle 9.7.1, AGP 9.4.1) and routine dependency
bumps (`go_router`, `file_picker`, lock file maintenance), plus a shorter,
user-first README. No user-visible change. Kept here for the technically
curious and for F-Droid's changelog.
