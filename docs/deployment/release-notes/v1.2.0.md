# Inner Flare 1.2.0: release notes

Release notes covering everything that's landed since
[v1.1.0](https://github.com/Health-Flare/InnerFlare/releases/tag/v1.1.0):
the unified dashboard grid, gauge and trend-chart cards, card resizing, the
cycle detail table, the auto-lock settings page, the first-run privacy and
disclaimer screen, and the fix for charts not refreshing, all merged to
`main`.

These are additive feature/UX changes, not a breaking change to anything
external, so this ships as a minor version (`1.2.0`).

Marketing copy and short-form video scripts built on these notes live in
`docs/marketing/v1.2.0/`.

## Full release notes

### One dashboard, shaped by you

Quick Stats, Calendar, and Insights used to live in separate sections. They
now share a single grid, and every card in it is a peer:

- **Drag to reorder** across the whole grid, not within separate areas.
- **Resize any card** from Customize dashboard by dragging its corner in the
  live preview. Cards can be half or full width and up to three rows tall;
  everything else reflows around the change as you make it.
- **Show or hide** anything, Calendar and Insights included.
- If your dashboard grows past six cards, Inner Flare suggests a tidy-up and
  points out duplicates by name. It's a suggestion, never an automatic
  change.

Layout choices are saved on this device only, like every other dashboard
preference.

### Gauge and trend-chart cards

Two new card types, added from **Customize dashboard → Add a card**:

- **Gauge**: days since your last period, or estimated days until your next
  one. The gauge fills relative to *your own* average cycle length, not a
  generic 28-day scale.
- **Trend chart**: your previous cycle lengths as a bar or line chart, with
  your average marked as a reference line and the most recent cycle
  highlighted.

Both are honest about thin data. With fewer than two complete cycles, or
cycles that vary a lot, the gauge shows a rough range labelled as such,
and the trend chart tells you it needs more history rather than drawing a
trend out of one data point.

More trend metrics (symptom frequency, flow intensity, cycle length
variability) are listed in the card catalog as "Coming soon". They aren't
tracked yet.

### A cycle table for your next appointment

Tap a trend card with at least two complete cycles and you get a plain
table: each cycle's start date, its length, and how many days it differs
from the one before, newest first. It shows what you logged and nothing
more: no interpretation, no diagnosis, no recommendation.

### Auto-lock has its own page

The idle re-lock timeout moved out of the main Settings list into its own
**Auto-lock** screen, with the same options as before, from "Immediately"
to "Never".

### A plain statement on first launch

The first time you unlock Inner Flare, it now tells you, before anything
else:

- It is not a medical or diagnostic device, and its predictions are
  estimates, not a diagnosis.
- Your logs stay on your device unless you explicitly export them.
- There is no account, email, or password to set up.

It shows once. You can re-read it any time from **Settings → Privacy and
disclaimer**, which also links to the published privacy policy. That link
opens in your browser only when you tap it; Inner Flare still has no
internet permission and does not fetch anything itself.

### Fixed: charts not updating

Dashboard charts could keep showing old numbers after you logged a day,
imported a backup, or restored data until you left and came back. They now
update immediately.

### Under the hood

Routine dependency and GitHub Actions updates, plus tidying such as
removing stray em dashes from user-facing text. No user-visible change
beyond that.
