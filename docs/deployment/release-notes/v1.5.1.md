# Inner Flare 1.5.1: release notes

A hotfix for 1.5.0: the Android app icon was blank on the home screen and
app drawer. No schema change, nothing removed, no other behaviour change.
Older backups still import. This is a patch version (`1.5.1`), build
number `9`.

---

## Store listing copy ("What's new")

Short version for Google Play (500-character per-locale limit) and App
Store "What's New in This Version":

> Fixed the app icon not showing on Android after the recent redesign.
> No other changes.

(96 characters, under the 500-character Play limit)

---

## Full release notes

### Fixed

- The Android app icon appeared blank on the home screen and in the app
  drawer after the 1.5.0 rebrand. The adaptive icon definition was
  missing its background and foreground layers, only a monochrome
  (themed-icon) layer was generated, which most launchers can't render
  on its own. The icon now has a full background, foreground and
  monochrome set, matching the rebranded Rose/white mark.

Nothing about logging, predictions, backups, or the lock screen has
changed. iOS is unaffected; this is an Android-only fix.
